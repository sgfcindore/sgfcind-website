What we offer

Our trucking services

End-to-end goods transportation solutions from Central India to every destination across the nation.

SGFC India operates a full-service trucking network from Madhya Pradesh, connecting Central India to Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu and beyond. We handle all types of goods — industrial, commercial, agricultural and consumer — with a fleet ranging from light 6-tyre vehicles to heavy 32-tyre trucks.

Full truckload (FTL)

MP → AP / TN / TS / All India

Your goods occupy the entire truck — no sharing, no intermediate stops. Ideal for large consignments requiring fast, direct delivery. We assign a dedicated vehicle from pickup to destination, ensuring maximum security and minimal transit time.

  • Direct point-to-point delivery
  • No intermediate handling
  • Faster transit times
  • Suitable for all goods types

Part load (LTL)

MP → All India corridors

Share truck space with other consignments and reduce your freight costs. Our consolidated load service runs on fixed schedules along our core MP to South India corridors, making it cost-effective for smaller shipments.

  • Cost-effective for smaller loads
  • Regular scheduled departures
  • Consolidated with other cargo
  • Tracked throughout transit

Dedicated route service

MP ↔ AP / TN / TS

Scheduled runs on the core corridors — Madhya Pradesh to Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and Tamil Nadu, and back the other way. The same lanes, on a timetable a shipper can plan around.

  • To and fro on the same lanes
  • Andhra Pradesh
  • Telangana
  • Tamil Nadu

Multi-axle heavy haulage

Pan India

For oversized, overweight, or industrial cargo that needs a heavy vehicle. 22-tyre and 32-tyre multi-axle trucks carry those loads across India, alongside the smaller trucks in the fleet.

  • 22-tyre vehicles
  • 32-tyre vehicles
  • Heavy and oversized loads
  • Movement across India

Container truck service

MP → All India / Ports

Secure, weatherproof container transport for goods requiring full enclosure and protection. Ideal for high-value cargo, packaged goods, electronics and export-quality consignments that need sealed, tamper-evident delivery.

  • Fully enclosed and weatherproof
  • Tamper-evident sealed containers
  • Ideal for high-value cargo
  • Export and import ready
