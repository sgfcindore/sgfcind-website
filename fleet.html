This is a trucking business. The vehicle is chosen for the goods: a small 6-tyre truck for a compact load, up to a 32-tyre multi-axle trailer when the cargo is heavy or oversized.

Ideal for smaller consignments, urban deliveries and time-sensitive cargo. Nimble and efficient for shorter routes and city access.

Best for

Up to 10 tonnes

Medium loads, highway routes, general cargo
