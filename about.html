About us

South Gujrat Freight Carrier (SGFC) is built on a legacy of trust, dedication, and excellence in transportation that began in 1993. With decades of experience in the trucking and freight transportation industry, we have grown through strong relationships, dependable service, and an unwavering commitment to our customers.

Over the years, we have continued to move forward with changing times while preserving the values that define us — integrity, reliability, and responsibility. Our journey is not just about transporting goods; it is about delivering promises, building lasting partnerships, and keeping businesses moving.

Our vision

To be a trusted and respected name in the transportation industry, setting new standards in reliability, efficiency, and customer satisfaction while connecting businesses and opportunities across the nation.

Our mission

To provide safe, timely, and dependable freight transportation through professional service, operational excellence, and a customer-first approach — ensuring every shipment is handled with care and every commitment is honoured.

Our commitment

At SGFC, every journey carries a promise. We believe in transparent communication, responsible handling, timely deliveries, and service that our customers can depend on. Our greatest achievement is the trust we have earned over the years, and our greatest ambition is to strengthen it with every delivery.

Since 1993 — A Legacy of Trust, Thirty-Plus Years of Excellence, and a Commitment to Every Journey.

Director’s note

A note from the director

At South Gujrat Freight Carrier, we believe every consignment carries the trust of a business. Our commitment is to deliver that trust — on time, every time, across every highway in India.

From Indore, we move all types of goods out of Madhya Pradesh, and we bring goods back the same way. A shipper in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, or Tamil Nadu can send freight north just as readily as a shipper in Madhya Pradesh can send it south.

The load decides the truck. A 6-tyre vehicle for a compact consignment. A multi-axle 22-tyre or 32-tyre trailer when the cargo is heavy or oversized. Full truckload, part load, and dedicated runs are the same promise: the goods leave with us, and they arrive as agreed.

M. N. V. Satyanarayana

Director, South Gujrat Freight Carrier (SGFC)
